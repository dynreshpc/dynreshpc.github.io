The Dynamic Resources for HPC Consortium is a collaborative group focused on advancing dynamic resource management, malleable applications, and adaptive scheduling in High-Performance Computing (HPC) systems.

The Consortium is governed by:

<div class="governance-cards">
  <div class="governance-card">
    <div class="governance-card__icon">👥</div>
    <h4>General Assembly</h4>
    <p>Composed of all members; primary decision‑making body.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="governance-card">
    <div class="governance-card__icon">🧭</div>
    <h4>Advisory Board</h4>
    <p>Up to one representative per participating institution, providing guidance.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="governance-card">
    <div class="governance-card__icon">⚙️</div>
    <h4>Leadership Board</h4>
    <p>Executes the Consortium’s guidelines and decisions.</p>
  </div>
</div>
