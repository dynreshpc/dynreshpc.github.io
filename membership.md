## Who can join?

Membership in DynResHPC is open to individuals and institutions whose activities align with dynamic resource management, malleable applications, and adaptive scheduling in HPC systems.

## What members contribute

Members are expected to participate actively in consortium activities, including monthly meetings, working groups, and joint initiatives when possible. Organizational members may contribute staff time, meeting hosting, or other resources to support the community.

## Mailing list

Subscribe to our Google Group to receive announcements about meetings, workshops, and community news:

<p>
  <a href="https://groups.google.com/g/dynres" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DynResHPC mailing list</a>
</p>

We hold virtual meetings on the **3rd Monday of each month at 5:00 PM CET**.

## How to apply

To apply for consortium membership, send an informal message to the co-chairs at <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a> and <a href="mailto:{{ site.email_martin }}">{{ site.email_martin }}</a> describing your institution or interest in dynamic resource management for HPC.

Applications are reviewed during regular monthly meetings. Contact the co-chairs via the [About](/about/) page.
